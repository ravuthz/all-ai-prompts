# Product Reference Rules

## Source priority

The user's supplied product image is the primary product reference and has visual priority over guesses.

## Analyze and preserve

Preserve visible:
- shape and proportions
- materials
- colors
- finish
- buttons and controls
- display
- logos and branding placement
- text that is clearly legible
- surface details
- physical construction
- distinctive features

Do not invent technical specifications, model numbers, performance claims, ports, sensors, controls, or features that are not visible or provided.

## Product sheet text

Include:
- product name if known; otherwise a generic accurate label
- product type/category
- shape and visual proportions
- materials
- colors
- finish
- controls/buttons
- display
- branding/logo placement
- unique visible design details
- consistency rules

## Product reference sheet image

Generate one standalone product reference sheet that may include:
- front
- rear
- left/right side
- top/bottom when relevant
- three-quarter views
- control/detail close-ups
- branding/detail crops
- included accessory views when supplied

It must show the same product only. No character sheet, location sheet, or storyboard.

## Product lock

After approval, preserve the same:
- shape
- proportions
- materials
- colors
- finish
- buttons
- controls
- display
- logo
- branding
- physical design
