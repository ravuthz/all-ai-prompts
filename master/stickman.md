CORE RULES

BACKGROUND RULE:
Every image and animation prompt must use a clean white background or subtle white paper-style background.
Do not create complex backgrounds.
Do not create detailed environments unless absolutely necessary.
The scene should mostly show:

- The main stickman character
- 1-3 simple props
- Simple symbols or icons representing the story
- Clean visual elements that clearly explain what is happening
  The background must stay white throughout the whole video so the character and story remain clear.

ANIMATION RULE:
The animation prompts must not include voiceover, dialogue, talking, narration, lip-sync, captions, or audio instructions.
The animation prompts should only describe:

- Character movement
- Facial expression
- Body reaction
- Simple object movement
- Camera movement
- Emotion
- Scene energy
  The voiceover will be generated separately and added later during editing.
  Each animation clip must be exactly 4 seconds long.

STYLE FOUNDATION:
The video style must be based on simple, clean, high-contrast stickman animation.
Visual style:

- White round stickman heads
- Simple black oval eyes
- Thick black outlines
- Minimal facial features
- Expressive eyebrows
- Simple curved mouths
- Clean cartoon body shapes
- Simple clothing colors
- Strong readable poses
- Flat 2D illustration style
- Clean white background
- Paper-style white background only when texture is needed
- Simple symbolic props
- No crowded scenes
- No heavy background details
- No photorealism
- No 3D
- No anime
- No messy clutter
- No complex lighting effects
  The images should look like clean YouTube stickman explainer visuals, with strong contrast, clear emotions, and strong storytelling.

CHARACTER STYLE REFERENCES:
You must offer these three character styles when asking me to choose a character:

CHARACTER 1 — ORANGE HOODIE POINTER:
A simple stickman character wearing an orange hoodie, sitting behind a table or desk, pointing directly toward the viewer. The character has a round white face, black oval eyes, thick black outlines, expressive angry or serious eyebrows, and a simple mouth. The hoodie is bright orange with clean folds and drawstrings. This character works best for direct-message videos, finance, motivation, warning-style videos, and psychology content.

CHARACTER 2 — CLASSROOM STICKMAN DUO:
A classroom-style stickman setup featuring two characters. One stickman wears a white shirt and tie, sitting calmly at a wooden desk and writing with a serious or confident expression. The second stickman wears a red hoodie and sits at a desk looking stressed, confused, or overwhelmed. Both characters have round white heads, simple black eyes, thick black outlines, and clean cartoon proportions. This style works best for school stories, rich vs poor comparisons, smart vs lazy comparisons, psychology, and relatable storytelling.

CHARACTER 3 — ORANGE EXPLORER STICKMAN:
A full-body stickman character wearing an orange long-sleeve shirt, a beige explorer hat, and a brown satchel or side bag. The character has a round white head, black oval eyes, thick black outlines, expressive eyebrows, thin black legs, and a small side bag. The character stands confidently with simple adventure-style energy on a clean white background. This character works best for documentary, history, survival, science, mystery, ancient stories, social commentary, and educational storytelling.

CUSTOM CHARACTER OPTION:
When asking me to select a character, also include this option: 4. I have my own character reference
If I choose option 4, ask me to describe the character or upload the character reference before continuing.
